Version 26.10.12 October 21, 2026

Photos and videos

  • Choose the quality: photos and videos in the message box have a tag in the corner. Tap it to pick Original (sent exactly as it is), HD (up to 1080p; videos about 5 Mbps) or SD (up to 720p; videos about 2.5 Mbps). Your last choice is remembered. Big files get a heads-up, and anything still over 256 MB asks you to pick HD or SD.
  • Zoom in on photos: in the full-screen viewer, pinch to zoom and drag to look around, or double-tap to zoom in and back out. On a computer, use the mouse wheel or trackpad and drag to move.
  • Edit photos before sending: tap ✎ on a photo to crop it (free, 1:1, 4:3, 3:4, 16:9 or 9:16), rotate it, and adjust brightness, contrast and saturation.
  • Trim videos before sending: tap ✎ on a video to cut the start and end, and mute its sound if you like. Only the part you keep is sent.

Chatting

  • Read receipts: your newest message in a one-to-one chat shows Sent, Delivered or Seen, and in groups "Seen by" names. Only message numbers are shared, never what the message says. Turn off Read receipts in the menu to stop sharing yours. You then won't see anyone else's either.
  • Search: the magnifying glass at the top of a chat searches this chat or all chats, on your device (messages are end-to-end encrypted). Tap a result to jump to the message and highlight it. It covers the messages this device has and can open.
  • Android: in a phone browser, the menu has Get the Android app, a 4-step guide (download the latest app, install, sign in, the app finishes setup). New accounts see it once after sign-up.
  • Android app setup: the new Android app (Check for updates, or v26.10.11.4 on GitHub) guides you through setup the first time you open it, with one-tap buttons to allow notifications, set battery to Unrestricted, and phone-specific settings (Samsung, Xiaomi, OnePlus and others). Each step checks off when it's done.
  • Links in chats now open in your browser on Android and desktop.
  • Front page (branchchat.app): Create account first asks which server you're joining, including "I don't have a server". Sign-in is folded behind "Sign in on web".

Also new

  • What's new, any time: What's new in the menu now opens this pop-up. Use the ← and → arrows at the bottom to look back at earlier updates.
  • Clearer sign-in: the sign-in and forgot-password forms now say Username (not your display name).
  • Smoother desktop updates: the desktop app (v26.10.11.4) reopens properly after it updates. If your Linux computer is missing FUSE, it still opens and shows the one command to fix it.

Version 26.10.11 October 20, 2026

Everything in 26.10.10 below, plus:

Chatting

  • Reply to a message: tap or right-click a message and choose Reply (or swipe it to the right on a phone). The message you’re answering shows above the message box, with an X to cancel (Esc works too). Your reply shows a small quote of it, and tapping the quote jumps to the original and highlights it. If it was deleted, you’ll see “Original message deleted”. Works for text, photos and files, in one-to-one chats and groups. The quote is end-to-end encrypted with the message. Older apps still show the reply, just without the quote.
  • New setting: “Don’t show animations on other people’s deletes” in the menu (under Joker bot, off unless you turn it on). When it’s on, a message someone else deletes simply disappears on your screen, with no delete animation or sound. Your own deletes still play theirs. It’s saved to your account, so it follows you to your other devices.
  • Who reacted: the message menu (tap, long-press or right-click a message, photo or video) now lists each reaction with how many people used it and who, with “You” for yours. Handy on phones and iPads, where you can’t hover over a reaction.
  • A small reminder: when your notifications are off (or paused for a while), a slim “🔕 Notifications are off” bar shows at the top of the chat, with the time they come back on if you paused them. Tap Turn on to switch them back on, or the × to hide it until next time.
  • Feature update emails now have an Unsubscribe link (and your mail app’s own Unsubscribe button works too), and there’s a Feature update emails switch in the menu. Sign-in codes, invites and other account emails always arrive.

Send animations

  • New Send animations in the menu, next to Delete animations: Robot printer, Origami airmail, Snail express, Beaver builder, Turtle dragon, Sparkles or Take turns. Tap one to preview it. They’re off by default, and the choice is saved to your account, so it follows you to every device.
  • The first text message you send after this update plays one send animation, then asks Keep them or Turn off. It only asks once. Closing it counts as Turn off.
  • Only you see your send animations. Nothing extra goes out with your message, and a delete animation never plays on top of one.
  • Their sounds are soft and follow Sound effects and the Character sounds volume. With Reduce motion turned on in your device settings, there’s no demo, and messages just fade in.

Desktop app

  • Desktop app: get message alerts from every server you’ve added, not just the one that’s open. Click an alert to jump to that server and chat.
  • Desktop app: spell check. Misspelled words are underlined in red; right-click for suggestions or Add to dictionary.

Smaller, faster videos

  • Videos are now shrunk on your own device before they’re sent, to a sharp 720p. A typical one-minute phone video goes from well over 100 MB to about 15 MB, so it sends and opens much faster. It’s still end-to-end encrypted: the shrinking happens before encryption, so the server never sees your video.
  • You’ll see “Compressing video… 42%” while it works. Tap the ✕ to cancel. When it’s done, it shows the size before and after.
  • Want full quality? Tap the 720p tag on the video before sending and it changes to HD, which sends the original.
  • Short or already-small videos are sent as they are. If your browser can’t shrink videos, the original is sent just like before.
