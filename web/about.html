About BranchChat

What BranchChat is

BranchChat is a small, private, invite-only chat for family and friends. Messages, photos, and videos are locked end to end, so only the people in the chat can read them.

Each BranchChat server is run by its owner, not by a big company's cloud. You can use it in your browser, or with the phone and desktop apps.

What matters here

  • Privacy by default. Your email and phone number stay hidden unless you choose to share them. No ads, no tracking.
  • A calm forest campsite look, like gathering around a fire.
  • Gentle sounds, which you can turn off any time.
  • A little fun, like the snapping helper that clears away deleted messages.
  • Easy on everyone: readable text, and less motion if your device asks for it.
  • Simple to get around: your chat list is home, and Back always brings you there.

Thanks for being here. Pull up a log by the fire.
