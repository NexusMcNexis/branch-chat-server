## Who can use BranchChat for free

LICENSE.md allows use for any **noncommercial purpose**. In its own words, permitted uses include:

- **Personal uses:** "Personal use for research, experiment, and testing for the benefit of public knowledge, personal study, private entertainment, hobby projects, amateur pursuits, or religious observance, without any anticipated commercial application."
- **Noncommercial organizations:** "Use by any charitable organization, educational institution, public research organization, public safety or health organization, environmental protection organization, or government institution ... regardless of the source of funding or obligations resulting from the funding."

In practice, we intend this to cover things like running your own BranchChat server for yourself, your family or your friends as a hobby, with no commercial purpose. If you're unsure whether your use counts, read LICENSE.md or ask.

## Small businesses can use it free too

BranchChat is also offered under the **PolyForm Small Business License 1.0.0** ([LICENSE-SMALL-BUSINESS.md](LICENSE-SMALL-BUSINESS.md)). You may choose whichever of the two licenses fits you. In its own words, a company may use BranchChat under it if the company "has fewer than 100 total individuals working as employees and independent contractors, and less than 1,000,000 USD (2019) total revenue in the prior tax year" (inflation-adjusted).

## Who needs a commercial license

Larger businesses (above the small-business limits) and anyone else using BranchChat for a commercial purpose not covered by either license (for example, running it inside a company, or offering it as a paid service) are **not** covered by LICENSE.md and need a separate commercial license from Luke Woods.

To ask about a commercial license:

CONTACT: licensing@branchchat.app

## Source-available, not "open source"

BranchChat's source code is public, but BranchChat is **source-available**, not "open source" as the Open Source Initiative (OSI) defines it, because the license restricts commercial use.

## Third-party components

BranchChat bundles or depends on third-party components, including (not a complete list) Fluent and Noto emoji, sound effects, Electron/Chromium and other dependencies. **Each of those keeps its own license.** LICENSE.md covers only Luke Woods' own work in BranchChat; it does not change the license of any third-party component.
